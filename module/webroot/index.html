Performance

The parts of the look that cost the most to draw. Each applies at once.

Glass mixes two pictures of what is behind it: the screen, and a blur of it. One draws a single blur in place of both (a single pass). Clear glass turns slightly soft, lighter frost loses the detail that showed through it, and tiles and cards on the shade show the shade's blur rather than a stronger one of their own.

The rainbow fringe where a pane's rim bends what is behind it. Off, the default, keeps the bend without the colours. On costs the most on a screen of small panes, such as a page of icons.

What clear glass shows through it. Quarter reads a quarter-size, slightly blurred copy of the screen in place of the screen itself, so the whole screen is not copied every frame.
